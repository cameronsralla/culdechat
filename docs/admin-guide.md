## 1. Why this exists (start here)
<!-- Expand from vision.md in plain operator language -->

Cul-de-Chat is **the modern digital town square for bringing local communities together** — for **one local community** at a time (a building, neighborhood, or similar place-based group). It exists because the modern internet pulled attention into global, low-context spaces while local neighbor connection got harder. This tool aims the network the other way: toward the people who share your place.

It is open source, non-commercial in spirit, and meant to be run by a community (or a volunteer/admin on their behalf). Each instance stands alone. There is no global feed of strangers.

**Your job as admin** is stewardship: who belongs, what’s official, and keeping the space healthy — not growing an audience.
